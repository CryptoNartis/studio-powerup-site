Privacy Policy: Boop the Blob

Last updated: October 4, 2026 · Version française

The short version

  • There is no account. Your game progress is saved on your device only.
  • We measure how the game is played with our own analytics, tied to a random install ID that is not linked to your name, email or advertising ID. You can turn this off in Settings → Share anonymous gameplay stats.
  • Ads are rewarded only: you choose to watch one for a bonus. Ads are served by Google AdMob and are family-friendly (G-rated) for everyone. They are personalized only if you say yes, and never for players under 15.
  • Purchases go through Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell your data for money. We do not share it for targeted advertising unless you allow personalized ads.

2. What we process and why

2.1 Your game save (stays on your device)

Your progress, settings, purchases you have received and your language choice are stored only on your device. We do not run accounts or cloud saves. We cannot see your save, and we cannot recover it if you delete the app or change phones.

2.2 The age question

Before we ask for any consent, the app asks for your year of birth, with no answer pre-selected. The year itself stays on your device. The app keeps a simple yes/no flag saying whether you are under 15, and uses it to switch off ad personalization, tracking prompts and notifications (see section 10). This flag is also attached to our analytics events (section 2.3) so that we can check those protections work.

2.3 Our own gameplay analytics

When you play, the app sends gameplay events to our own database (hosted by Supabase in the European Union, in Paris). Examples of events: a session started, a tutorial step was completed, you reached a level or unlocked an island, a sticker dropped, you asked for a rewarded ad and whether it finished, a purchase was started, completed or cancelled (product name only, never payment details), whether you accepted or refused the ad consent and tracking prompts (the answer only, never your advertising ID), and error reports when something goes wrong.

Feedback you choose to send. If you write to Boop with "Tell Boop something" in Settings, the text you type (up to 500 characters) is sent to the same database with your random install ID, so we can read and act on it. Please don't include personal details. It is kept for 13 months like other events, and you can ask us to delete it.

Each event carries:

  • a random install ID created on your device the first time you open the app. It is not your advertising ID (IDFA), not your device's serial number, and it is not linked to your name, email, Apple ID or any other identity. It is shown at the bottom of Settings in the app; tap it to copy it if you contact us. A new one is generated after 13 months;
  • a random session ID that changes each time you start playing;
  • the app version and build number, the platform (iOS), your device's operating system version and your language/region setting (locale);
  • the under-15 flag described above;
  • the in-game context of the event (for example your level and island).

Why: to measure our audience (how many people play, which features they use, where they get stuck), to balance the game, and to find and fix crashes and errors. We use these figures only in aggregate, only for ourselves, and we never combine them with other data sources or use them for advertising.

You can switch this off at any time with the Share anonymous gameplay stats switch in Settings. The app then stops sending these events and discards any that were waiting to be sent.

When the app connects to our database, our hosting provider necessarily receives your device's IP address as part of the connection. We do not store it in our analytics tables. Our provider keeps short-lived technical logs (including IP addresses) for security and troubleshooting, for a few days only (see section 6).

2.4 Remote configuration

The app downloads game settings from the same database (for example how many bonus ads are available per day). This download contains no personal data about you; as with any connection, your IP address is seen by our host.

2.5 Game updates (Expo)

When the app starts, it checks the servers of Expo (EAS Update) for a newer version of the game files, so that we can deliver bug fixes without waiting for a full App Store update. To answer that check, Expo receives your IP address, technical information about the app (app and runtime version, platform, the update currently installed) and a random technical identifier created by the update component. That identifier is not our install ID or your advertising ID. We use this only to deliver fixes and to see how many devices have received an update.

2.6 Rewarded ads (Google AdMob)

The app shows rewarded ads only: a video plays only when you tap a ▶ button to get a bonus. There are no banners and no ads that interrupt play. Ads are served by Google AdMob, and the app asks Google for family-friendly (G-rated) ads for every player.

Consent first. After the tutorial, Google's User Messaging Platform (a certified consent tool under the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework) shows a consent form where the law requires one, for example in the European Economic Area and the UK. "Refuse all" is as easy as "Accept all". Then, for players aged 15 or over, Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt asks whether the app may use your advertising ID. It appears only if you accepted Google's form, or if you are in a region where that form is not required (such as the United States).

Depending on your choices, Google may collect:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allowed tracking in the Apple prompt;
  • your IP address (which can indicate your approximate location, at city or country level);
  • ad interaction data (which ad was shown, whether you watched it to the end or tapped it);
  • diagnostic data about the ad SDK's performance and crashes;
  • your consent choices, stored on your device as a consent string.

What changes with your choice:

  • If you accept Google's form (where it is shown) or allow tracking in the Apple prompt, Google may use this data to show you ads based on your interests and to measure them.
  • If you refuse Google's form, Google shows only non-personalized or limited ads. If you do not allow tracking, Google cannot use your advertising ID. In both cases Google still processes some technical data (such as your IP address) to deliver the ad, count it, cap how often it repeats and prevent fraud.
  • For players under 15, the app asks Google for non-personalized ads only, tags every ad request as coming from a user under the age of consent, and never shows the Apple tracking prompt.

Google processes ad data under its own privacy policy, as an independent controller for some purposes: policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses data from apps that use its services. Where the consent form is shown, it lists Google and any other ad technology providers involved; you can review and change your choices at any time (section 7).

2.7 In-app purchases (Apple)

Purchases (gem packs, the starter pack, ad-free bonuses and the monthly Goo Pass premium) are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles your payment, billing details and receipts under its own privacy policy. We never receive your name, card details or Apple ID. The app receives a confirmation that a product was paid for, delivers the item on your device, and records a "purchase" analytics event with the product name (section 2.3).

2.8 Notifications

If you agree in the app and then in the iOS permission prompt, the app schedules local notifications on your device (for example "your goo jar is full" or "your daily gift is ready"). They are created and stored on your device: no notification token or data is sent to us. At most 2 per day, never between 22:00 and 09:00 your local time. Players under 15 are not offered notifications at all. You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings.

2.9 When you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address, your message and anything you choose to include (such as your install ID) to answer you and handle your request.

2.10 Information Apple shares with us

If you have chosen in iOS settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us crash reports and aggregated statistics (such as installs or sessions by country). These come from Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and we cannot identify you from them.

2.11 What we do not do

  • No account, no login, no contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.
  • No sale of personal data for money.
  • No sensitive personal information (as defined by US state privacy laws) is collected.
  • No automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

4. Who receives data

Only the following organizations receive personal data from the app, and only for the purposes above:

  • Supabase (Supabase Inc.), our database host, acting as our processor (service provider). Data is stored in the EU (AWS Paris region, eu-west-3). Supabase also hosts some of the game's sound files: when you visit an island, the app downloads that island's sounds from Supabase, which sees your IP address as part of that download (nothing else is sent, and nothing is stored about you).
  • Expo (650 Industries, Inc., United States), which delivers game updates (section 2.5), acting as our processor (service provider).
  • Google (Google LLC, and Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA and UK), for AdMob ads and the consent form. Google acts as our processor for some processing and as an independent controller (a third party) for others, as described in Google's terms.
  • Apple (Apple Inc., and Apple Distribution International Ltd. in the EEA and UK), which distributes the app, processes purchases and shows the tracking prompt, as an independent controller.

We may also disclose data if required by law or a court order, or to a successor if our business is transferred. We do not sell or rent personal data to anyone for money.

5. International transfers

We are based in the United States. If you play from the EEA or the UK, the data the app sends is collected directly by us under the GDPR or UK GDPR. Our analytics database stays in the EU (Paris); we access it from the United States.

  • Supabase: data is stored in the EU. Supabase Inc. is based in the United States, so its staff may access data for support or security; this is covered by the Standard Contractual Clauses in Supabase's data processing agreement.
  • Expo: update checks are handled by Expo in the United States, under Expo's data processing terms.
  • Google: data may be processed in the United States. Google LLC is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (and its UK extension), and Google also uses the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Apple: Apple may transfer data to Apple Inc. in the United States under its own safeguards (see Apple's privacy policy).

You can ask us for more information on these safeguards using the contact details below.

7. Your choices in the app

  • Settings → Share anonymous gameplay stats: switch our gameplay analytics off or on.
  • Settings → Privacy choices: reopen Google's ad consent form to accept or refuse personalized ads, where it is available in your region.
  • iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking: allow or stop tracking for Boop the Blob at any time. Turning tracking off stops Google from using your advertising ID for personalized ads.
  • iOS Settings → Notifications → Boop the Blob: turn notifications off.
  • Your install ID is shown at the bottom of Settings. Tap it to copy it, so you can paste it into an email to us.
  • Deleting the app removes your save, your install ID and your choices from the device. To also delete analytics already sent to us, email us your install ID before you uninstall.

8. Your rights in the EEA and UK

Under the GDPR and the UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • access the data we hold about you, and get a copy;
  • rectify inaccurate data;
  • erase your data;
  • restrict how we use it;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interest, including analytics;
  • data portability: receive the data you gave us in a common machine-readable format;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting what was done before.

Some countries give you extra rights; for example, in France you can give instructions about what happens to your data after your death.

To use these rights, see how to make a request below. If you think we have not respected your rights, you can complain to the data protection authority of the country where you live or work (list: edpb.europa.eu), or in the UK to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

10. Children and teens

Boop the Blob is made for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, but it is suitable for all ages, so we protect younger players by default:

  • A neutral year-of-birth question is asked before any consent request.
  • For players under 15: no ad consent form, no Apple tracking prompt, no tracking, non-personalized ads only, every ad request tagged as coming from a user under the age of consent, and no notifications offered.
  • Our gameplay analytics (section 2.3) still runs for them unless switched off, with the same limits as for everyone: random install ID only, no identity, no advertising use. It is used only to keep the game working and improve it.
  • Purchases require the Apple account holder's approval according to the device's settings. Parents can use Screen Time and Family Sharing "Ask to Buy" to control purchases.

Children under 13 (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 beyond what the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act allows without parental consent to support the internal operations of the app. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 in any other way, we will delete it.

Parents or legal guardians can contact us at Anastase@studio-powerup.com, with the install ID shown in the app's Settings, to have their child's analytics data deleted.

11. Security

Data sent by the app travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to our database is restricted and protected. The install ID cannot be used to identify you on its own. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep what we collect small so that there is little to lose.
