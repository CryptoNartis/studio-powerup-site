Help & support

Boop the Blob, a Pocket Gremlin game by Studio Power-Up LLC · questions, problems, data requests

Write to us

Email Anastase@studio-powerup.com. A real person reads every message, usually within a few days. It helps a lot if you include:

  • your install ID (in the app: Settings, at the bottom; tap it to copy it);
  • your device model and iOS version;
  • the app version (also at the bottom of Settings);
  • what happened, and a screenshot if you have one.

Purchases

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Settings → Restore purchases (or Shop → Store → Restore purchases) while signed in to the same Apple account you bought with. This brings back the starter pack and ad-free bonuses.

Gem packs and Goo Pass premium are consumable: they are used up when delivered and stored only in your save on the device, so Apple cannot restore them after the app is deleted. See "I lost my progress" below.

I paid but did not get my item

Close the app completely and reopen it with an internet connection: pending purchases are delivered at launch. If it still has not arrived, email us with your install ID, the product name and the date (Apple's receipt email has both). We will sort it out.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue App Store refunds ourselves, but if something did not work, tell us so we can fix it.

Is the Goo Pass premium a subscription?

No. It is a one-time purchase for the current calendar month's season and it never renews on its own. Its rewards are fixed and shown before you buy. The season ends at the end of the month, so buying early in the month gives you the most time to reach the tiers.

I am a parent. How do I control purchases?

On iPhone and iPad, use Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions → iTunes & App Store Purchases, or turn on Ask to Buy in Family Sharing. Players who say they are under 15 never see personalized ads, tracking prompts or notification requests.

Progress and saves

I lost my progress / I changed phones

Boop the Blob has no account and no cloud save yet: your progress is stored only on your device. If the app was deleted or the phone was reset or replaced, the save is gone and we have no copy on our side, so we cannot bring it back. Restore purchases will return the starter pack and ad-free bonuses.

A save restored from a full iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) may bring your progress back with it.

Can I start over?

Yes: Settings → Reset progress. This cannot be undone. Restorable purchases can be restored again afterwards.

Ads

I watched an ad but did not get the bonus

The bonus is given when the video finishes. If it was closed early or failed to load, nothing is lost and you can try again (bonus ads are limited per day). If you are sure the video finished, email us with your install ID and the time.

How do I change my ad, tracking and stats choices?

Ads: in the app, Settings → Privacy choices (where available in your region). Tracking: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Gameplay statistics: the Share anonymous gameplay stats switch in the app's Settings.

US players: keeping tracking off is how you opt out of the "sharing" of your data for targeted ads. See the privacy policy.

Your data

What data does the game collect?

Gameplay statistics tied to a random install ID (not your name, email or advertising ID), a check for game updates with Expo when the app starts, and, for ads, what Google AdMob collects according to your choices. The full details are in the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?
  1. Open Settings in the app and tap your install ID (at the bottom) to copy it.
  2. Email Anastase@studio-powerup.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and your install ID. We delete the analytics linked to that ID and confirm by email, within one month at most.
  3. Uninstall the app. This removes your save, your install ID and your choices from the device.

Do step 1 before uninstalling: without the install ID we cannot find your data, because we do not know who you are. You can also switch off gameplay statistics at any time with Settings → Share anonymous gameplay stats. For data held by Google for ads, see Google's My Ad Center.

Notifications

Notifications are optional, at most 2 a day and never between 22:00 and 09:00. Turn them off in iOS Settings → Notifications → Boop the Blob.

En français

Boop the Blob est un jeu Pocket Gremlin de Studio Power-Up LLC. Écrivez-nous à Anastase@studio-powerup.com en indiquant votre identifiant d'installation (en bas des Réglages du jeu ; touchez-le pour le copier), votre modèle d'appareil, la version d'iOS et la version de l'application.

Restaurer mes achats

Réglages → Restaurer les achats, avec le même compte Apple que lors de l'achat. Le pack de départ et les bonus sans pub sont restaurés. Les packs de gemmes et le Goo Pass premium sont consommables : ils ne peuvent pas être restaurés après la suppression de l'application.

J'ai perdu ma progression

Il n'y a pas encore de compte ni de sauvegarde dans le cloud : la progression est stockée uniquement sur votre appareil. Si l'application a été supprimée ou l'appareil réinitialisé, nous n'en avons aucune copie et ne pouvons pas la récupérer. Une sauvegarde complète de l'iPhone (iCloud ou ordinateur) peut la contenir.

Remboursement

Les remboursements App Store sont gérés par Apple sur reportaproblem.apple.com. Prévenez-nous aussi si un achat n'est pas arrivé.

Publicités, suivi et statistiques

Publicités : Réglages → Choix de confidentialité dans le jeu (là où c'est disponible dans votre région). Suivi : Réglages d'iOS → Confidentialité et sécurité → Suivi. Statistiques de jeu : l'interrupteur Partager des statistiques de jeu anonymes dans les Réglages du jeu.

Supprimer mes données
  1. Dans les Réglages du jeu, touchez votre identifiant d'installation pour le copier.
  2. Envoyez-le à Anastase@studio-powerup.com avec l'objet « Demande de suppression ». Nous supprimons les statistiques liées à cet identifiant et vous le confirmons par e-mail, sous un mois maximum.
  3. Désinstallez l'application : la sauvegarde, l'identifiant et vos choix sont effacés de l'appareil.

Faites l'étape 1 avant de désinstaller. Vous pouvez aussi désactiver les statistiques à tout moment avec Réglages → Partager des statistiques de jeu anonymes. Détails : politique de confidentialité.
